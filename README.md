# Data Professional Survey Dashboard

## About the Project

This project analyzes survey responses from **630 data professionals** to better understand their careers, salaries, preferred programming languages, and overall job satisfaction.

I used **Power BI** to clean and organize the survey data, then built an interactive dashboard to make the results easier to explore and compare.

## What I Analyzed

The dashboard looks at questions such as:

- What are the most common roles in the data field?
- Which programming languages are most popular?
- How do salaries compare across different job titles?
- How difficult is it to break into a data career?
- How satisfied are data professionals with their salary and work-life balance?
- Where are survey participants located?

## Main Findings

- **Data Analyst** was the most common role in the survey, representing about 60% of respondents.
- **Python** was the most popular programming language, chosen by about two-thirds of participants.
- The average age of survey respondents was around **30 years old**.
- About **32%** of respondents said breaking into data was difficult or very difficult.
- Respondents rated their **work-life balance higher than their salary satisfaction**, suggesting compensation may be a bigger concern than work-life balance for this group.
- Salary levels varied across different data-related job titles.

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Average salary by job title
- Favorite programming language by role
- Difficulty of entering the data field
- Salary satisfaction
- Work-life balance satisfaction
- Country distribution of survey respondents
- Total survey participants and average age
